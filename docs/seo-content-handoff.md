Every horse is groomed, checked and exercised as part of a fixed daily routine, not only on the days it is ridden.

Calm handling on the ground is what produces calm horses under saddle. Horses that are used to being groomed, leading and picked up are the ones that are not surprised under a rider.

Feed, exercise and rest are managed by staff who work with the same horses every day, and who know the signs that a horse is not quite right.

#### Questions and answers

These answers appear when a visitor opens a question on the Contact page.

**Getting Started**

**What is the minimum age to start horse riding?**

Children can begin riding with us from 5 years of age and above. Training is planned according to the rider’s age, confidence and ability.

**Is there an upper age limit for adults?**

No. We do not have a fixed upper age limit for adult riders. Adults can take up horse riding based on their comfort and suitability for the activity.

**Is there a weight limit for horse riding?**

Yes. For the safety and well-being of both the rider and the horse, our current rider weight limit is below 75 kg.

**Do I need previous horse-riding experience to join?**

Not at all. We welcome complete beginners as well as experienced riders. Our training is structured according to each rider’s current ability, confidence and riding goals.

**Can children try horse riding before joining regular classes?**

Yes. Children aged 5 years and above can begin with a Guest Ride or introductory riding experience before deciding on regular training.

**What should I wear for my first riding session?**

We recommend comfortable, fitted clothing and suitable closed footwear. Our team can guide you regarding appropriate riding attire and safety equipment when you book your session.

**Can I visit the club before enrolling?**

Yes. Prospective riders and families are welcome to visit the club to understand our facilities and training environment. We recommend contacting us in advance to arrange your visit.

**How do I know which riding program is suitable for me or my child?**

Our team can help you choose the appropriate program based on the rider’s age, previous riding experience, confidence and goals, whether the objective is recreational riding, structured learning or competitive training.

**Programs and Training**

**How long does it take to learn horse riding?**

Learning time varies from rider to rider depending on factors such as consistency, confidence and individual progress. On average, a rider may require approximately 45-50 classes to develop the basic skills of walk, trot and canter. Further training is required to progress into more advanced riding and specific equestrian disciplines.

**Do you offer Guest Rides or one-time horse-riding experiences?**

Yes. We offer Guest Rides / Horse Experience sessions for individuals and families who would like to experience horse riding without initially enrolling in a regular training program. Advance booking is recommended.

**Which equestrian disciplines do you offer?**

Our training includes Show Jumping, Dressage, general riding and horsemanship, with structured development based on the rider’s level and goals.

**Do you provide competitive equestrian training?**

Yes. We provide structured training for riders interested in progressing towards equestrian competitions, with emphasis on riding technique, horse-rider partnership and progressive development.

**Do I need to own a horse to learn riding?**

No. You do not need to own a horse to begin training. Suitable trained horses are allocated based on the rider’s experience, ability and training requirements.

**Can experienced riders join for advanced or competitive training?**

Yes. Experienced riders can join based on their current riding level and goals. Our team can assess their requirements and recommend an appropriate training program.

**Horses and Safety**

**How are horses assigned to riders?**

Horses are assigned considering the rider’s experience, ability, confidence and training objectives. An appropriate horse-rider combination is important for both safety and effective learning.

**Do you have an indoor riding arena?**

Yes. We have a covered indoor riding arena, providing a comfortable and controlled environment for regular equestrian training.

**Visiting and Booking**

**How can I book a riding session?**

You can contact us through the enquiry details provided on our website. Our team will assist you with available sessions, suitable programs and the booking process.
